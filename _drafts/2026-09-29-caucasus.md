In May of 2026 my friend Zsolt and I spent two weeks birding through Georgia and Armenia. Both of us had been to the Caucasus before, Zsolt a couple times to Georgia and me once to Armenia. However, neither of us had done much birding on these trips and were both keen to do the birdlife of the Caucasus 'properly'.

# Target species

The main attractions here include the specialities of the high Caucasus: Great rosefinch _Carpodacus rubicilla_, the endemic Caucasian snowcock _Tetraogallus caucasicus_, and the striking Güldenstädt's redstart _Phoenicurus erythrogastrus_. At slightly lower elevations, one can find the endemic Caucasian grouse _Lyrurus mlokosiewiczi_, Crimson-winged finch _Rhodopechys sanguineus_, Radde's accentor _Prunella ocularis_, White-throated robin _Irania gutturalis_, and the Caspian snowcock _T. caspius_. In the green beech woodlands below, there are Green warblers _Phylloscopus nitidus_, Semicollared flycatcher _Ficedula semitorquata_, and Red-breasted flycatcher _Ficedula parva_. And in the drier country, one can encounter Black francolin _Francolinus francolinus_, Desert Finch _Rhodospiza obsoleta_, and Blue-cheeked bee-eater _Merops persicus_.
